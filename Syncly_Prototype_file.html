SynclyWeb prototype of the Android app

Campus placement notices arrive as a flood of WhatsApp messages. Syncly reads them as they land, turns them into event cards, and files every company into its own vault, so nothing is missed.

How a notice becomes an event

  1. 1
    Notification capturedAndroid's NotificationListener hands Syncly every WhatsApp preview.
  2. 2
    Group whitelistOnly chats whose names match your keywords (e.g. ME UG Placements) are read. The rest are ignored.
  3. 3
    Regex extractor (offline)Finds company, type, date, time, venue, links and points on the device, with no internet needed.
  4. 4
    Gemini fallbackIf regex can't get full context (messy text, "around 6ish"), your own Gemini key fills the gaps.
  5. 5
    Synced or PendingDate and time both known → Synced. Either missing → Pending until you tap Update.
  6. 6
    Company VaultA new company gets its own vault for PPT notes, images, PDFs and links.

Try it yourself

    Why the real app is Android-native

    Only Android lets an app read other apps' notifications (NotificationListenerService), which is what makes hands-free WhatsApp parsing possible. iOS does not allow this.

    Installing the APK means enabling developer options, turning off Play Protect and allowing unknown sources. So this browser version runs the same pipeline, with the Generate notification button standing in for real WhatsApp traffic.
